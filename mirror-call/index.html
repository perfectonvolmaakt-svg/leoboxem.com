What This Call Is

A Space to Look
Honestly

Private. With love. Without judgment.

Your Mirror of Meaning Call is a private 30 to 45 minute conversation with Leo — on Zoom, just the two of you.

Together, we will take an honest look at your life. Your relationships, your health, your finances, your time, your sense of purpose.

Not to judge. Not to fix. But to see clearly.

We will explore where you feel truly alive — and where you don't. And then, with love and gentleness, I will ask you the question most people never allow themselves to face:

"If this were the end of your life — would you have lived enough?
Have you been who you truly are?
Have you done what you are here to do?"
What We Will Do Together

The Call,
Step by Step

We look at the full picture
I will ask about every area of your life — relationships, health, time, finances, purpose and work. Not as a checklist, but as a living map of where you actually are.
We find where you are truly alive
We look honestly at where you feel fulfilled — and where you don't. Where your energy flows freely, and where something quietly drains.
I ask permission to hold up the mirror
I will ask if you want me to be honest with you. If you say yes, I will show you what I see — with love, and without softening what needs to be said.
"If this were the end of your life — would you have lived enough?
Have you been who you truly are? Have you done what you are here to do?
Have you truly lived?"
We explore what is missing — and what is in the way
If the answer is not yet — we get honest about why. What has been stopping you? What are you waiting for? What have you been telling yourself?
We get to your true desire
What is it that you actually want? Not what seems responsible, or realistic, or safe. What does your gut know? What has it always known?
If it feels right — I share how I can help
If you want to move forward and feel I can support you, I will share what working together could look like. You decide. No pressure, no rush. The call always stands on its own.
