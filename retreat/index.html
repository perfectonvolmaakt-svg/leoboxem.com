The Art of Being & Becoming

Growing into new dimensions of life, leadership and purpose requires us to deepen two capacities at the same time:

BE Presence & embodiment
BECOME Vision & direction

Like breathing in and breathing out.
Like ebb and flow.

Without vision, direction and intention, we do not move forward consciously.

But without embodiment, self-awareness and inner alignment, we often find ourselves running in many directions at once.

Busy. Productive. Successful perhaps. Yet disconnected from ourselves.

The Be & Becoming Retreat™ was created to help you remember how to do both.
To be fully present with where you are.
And to consciously become who you are here to be.

Why Ibiza?

Beyond its beauty lies something deeper.

The island carries a unique energy of liberation, transformation, creativity and renewal.

Many people describe arriving here as if something inside them immediately exhales.

And that is exactly why this retreat takes place here.

Because Ibiza is not simply the backdrop.

It becomes part of the journey.

Sacred Places & Transformational Experiences

Ibiza as teacher.

Throughout the retreat we will work not only within our sessions, but also with the island itself. Depending on the needs of the group, the weather, the season and what will best support the process unfolding, Leo will guide the group to carefully selected locations across the island. Each place carries its own energetic quality, symbolic meaning and invitation.

Tanit Cave
A place connected to feminine wisdom, fertility, life force, protection, regeneration and sacred becoming.
Es Vedrà
A place associated with transformation, manifestation, possibility and feminine power.
The Lighthouse Walk

A place of perspective, vision and direction.

An invitation to stop running around and instead become the lighthouse.

Grounded. Steady. Clear.

Pou des Lleó
A place connected to authenticity, courage, resilience and the untamed spirit of the island. A reminder that our deepest strength often emerges when we return to what is most true.

Rather than following a rigid itinerary, we allow the retreat to unfold in service of the women present.
Because transformation is rarely linear. And the most powerful experiences are often the ones we could not have planned.

Location & Stay

Your own apartment.
Directly at the sea.

During the five days of the retreat, you will stay in your own private apartment — situated directly at the sea.

Not a hotel room. Not a shared villa. Your own space, where you can return after each session to rest, to journal, or simply sit on your terrace and listen to the water.

The accommodation is priced at local rate — a genuine, beautiful location that remains accessible and unhurried in October.

Food

All meals during the retreat are included. We eat simply, beautifully and well — fresh, local, Mediterranean. Food that nourishes your body and keeps your energy clear and open throughout the five days together.
