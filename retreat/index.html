The philosophy

The Art of Being & Becoming

Growing into new dimensions of life, leadership and purpose requires us to deepen two capacities at the same time:

BE — Presence & Embodiment

Coming home to yourself. Being fully here. Listening inward.

BECOME — Vision & Direction

Moving forward consciously. Knowing where you are going. Creating what wants to emerge.

Like breathing in and breathing out. Like ebb and flow.

Without vision, direction and intention, we do not move forward consciously. But without embodiment, self-awareness and inner alignment, we often find ourselves running in many directions at once.

Busy. Productive. Successful perhaps. Yet disconnected from ourselves.

The Be & Becoming Retreat™ was created to help you remember how to do both. To be fully present with where you are. And to consciously become who you are here to be.

Location & stay

Your Own Apartment. Directly at the Sea.

Not a hotel room. Not a shared villa. Your own space.

During the five days of the retreat, you will stay in your own private apartment — situated directly at the sea. Your own space, where you can return after each session to rest, to journal, or simply sit on your terrace and listen to the water.

All meals during the retreat are included. Your fridge will be filled with breakfast and snacks to your liking. Leo will take you out for lunches and dinners — a combination of rest and having your own space, with a sense of togetherness, community and celebration. Exactly what this retreat needs.
