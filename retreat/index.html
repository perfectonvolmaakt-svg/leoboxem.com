Sacred Places & Transformational Experiences

Ibiza as teacher.

Depending on the needs of the group, the season, the weather and what is most supportive for your process, Leo will guide the group to carefully selected locations across the island. Each place carries its own symbolic meaning and energetic quality.

Tanit Cave
A sacred place dedicated to Tanit, the ancient goddess of Ibiza. Associated with feminine power, creation, fertility, intuition and rebirth.
Es Vedrà
One of the most magnetic and mystical places in the world. A powerful symbol of transformation, manifestation, feminine energy and possibility.
Lighthouse Walk
A place of vision, perspective and direction. An invitation to rise above the noise and reconnect with what truly matters.
Pou des Lleó
A place connected to courage, authenticity, resilience and the untamed spirit of the island. A reminder that your deepest strength often emerges when you return to what is most true.

Rather than following a fixed schedule, we allow the retreat to unfold in service of the women present. This creates a deeply personal and transformative experience.
